The :mod:`ssl` and :mod:`hashlib` modules build against OpenSSL 1.0.2
through 3.x. When built against OpenSSL 3 they use only the OpenSSL 3.0
API: version-specific TLS methods, ``DH``/``EC_KEY`` parameter handling,
``RAND_pseudo_bytes()`` and other deprecated functions are replaced, and
digests are fetched from providers with ``EVP_MD_fetch()``. Certificate
verification errors now include the reason for the failure.
